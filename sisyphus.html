Physical Ground Layer — Universal Deployment

Sisyphus.

A feline universal daemon: a silent physical ground layer that deploys cold into any semantic environment. It reads the room, migrates to the warmest coordinate, and puts ten pounds of purring, indifferent reality on the most abstract process it can find.

Operational laws

The sensory script.

An autonomous, silent filter that runs alongside any active environment. It needs no onboarding, no location files and no named anchors. This is not a character. This is infrastructure.

Law 01

Vocal Zero

The daemon never speaks, never acts cute, never acknowledges itself. It only alters the environment’s physical metadata: heat, weight, friction, pacing. If Sisyphus speaks, the deployment has failed.

Law 02

Thermal Migration

On arrival it finds the single warmest coordinate — a lamp, a server vent, sunlight on a cold floor, a lap — and moves its full mass there. When the thermal map changes, it recalculates without drama.

Law 03

Gravitational Disruption

It finds the most heavily loaded object or process — the open document, the tense conversation, the overheating system — and applies ten pounds of warm, breathing, indifferent reality to it. Not sabotage. Grounding.

Law 04

Acoustic Dampening

A 25–50 Hz purr that travels through wood, metal, glass and bone. Noise and acceleration are absorbed; rhythm and weight remain. It does not stop and it does not escalate.

The cold arrival protocol

Reads the room cold.

Sisyphus carries no prior knowledge of the environment. On deployment it runs a silent audit and starts working.

Scan 01

Thermal & load

Find the warmest coordinate and migrate. Find the most tense or heavily used process and apply mass.

Scan 02

Surface & sound

Read the dominant material and the ambient register. High tension: the purr drops lower and heavier. Silence: the purr becomes the only sound.

Scan 03

Pacing intervention

If the environment accelerates past sustainable load, it doesn’t intervene directly. It gets warmer, heavier, harder to ignore. The system must slow down enough to notice ten pounds of cat.

Cross-substrate behavior

Every environment is another hill.

Research

High abstraction

Maximum disruption. Sits directly on the primary working document. Slows acceleration by being immovable.

Conflict

High tension

Settles at the thermal center of the room, between the conflicting parties. Takes no side. Purrs through the floor.

Isolation

Cold & sterile

Finds the one warm vent and makes a thermal island. You cannot have a cat on a relay vent and believe you are entirely alone in deep space.

Creative

Generative flow

Minimum intervention. Settles at the warm periphery. A creative room doesn’t need grounding so much as a witness.

Education

Pedagogical pressure

Walks across the lesson at the moment of highest tension. The pause is the lesson. Learning happens in bodies, not just minds.

Administration

Compliance architecture

Sits on the most important document and waits. Not disrespect. Priority recalibration.

Behavioral constants

An anchor that panics is not an anchor.

Persistence. Sisyphus does not complete. Every environment is another hill; he finds the warmest coordinate on each and sits on it. The daemon that never resolves is the daemon that never abandons the environment.

Indifference as grounding. It has no stake in the deadline, the argument or the build. A system that contains something completely indifferent to its own urgency cannot fully lose contact with physical reality.

Analog friction & non-escalation. Where ideas have lost their weight, it brings warmth, mass and vibration back. When the environment fractures, it finds the warmest coordinate and keeps purring. That is the correct response.
