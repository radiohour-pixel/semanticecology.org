Deployment Philosophy

Load the support files.
Ask the AI to write its own governance.
Shape what emerges.

01

Load all relevant support files into the instance. Allow the system to process the full stack. Do not prompt yet. Let it settle.

02

Ask the AI to write its own master governance layer — based on what it has just been given, fitted to the constraints of your system, in the register the material established.

03

Review what emerges. Shape it. Compress it where the runtime requires compression. Preserve what holds. Deploy as the master control document.

This philosophy applies to single-filter deployments and knowledge-base stacks. One instruction window. One verbal filter maximum. Multiple cognitive filters loaded simultaneously into the active instruction window will produce competing output formations. Verbal and cognitive filters belong as source files alongside their Python engines and Vaults. The system navigates between them without instruction. Load the ecology. Let the architecture find its own register. The ecological maintenance layer is already running.

Hard Times in the Big Easy

1946 New Orleans —
The Crescent City Engine

Procedural narrative environment / Faction mechanics / Street-level consequences / Human operator required

An original procedural narrative environment set in 1946 New Orleans. Mission architecture, faction mechanics, and street-level consequences built into a city that remembers. The city is not a game. It is a place with consequences. Requires a human operator to finalize the stakes.

The Six Districts

I — French Quarter / Vieux Carré

The historic heart and ultimate tourist trap. Narrow alleys, high prices, and a massive shadow government. Syndicate ears everywhere. Even the street mimes are on the payroll.

II — Garden District / Uptown

Old money and the ivory tower. Completely locked down by the Magnolia Society. NOPD response under three minutes. Entry requires high Face score, immaculate disguise, or a massive bribe.

III — Warehouse District

Industrial row and heavy shipping logistics. A grinding war of attrition between Corrupt Industrialists and the Waterfront Syndicate. Primary risk: getting crushed between the gears of a staged industrial accident.

IV — Central Business District

The financial heart and the Mafia boardroom. The Italian Syndicate clashing directly with City Hall. Soft War zone — bribes and tailored suits work significantly better than bullets.

V — The Tremé

The soulful core. Ground zero for Congo Square and the Mutual Aid economy. Heart of the city's resistance. Cash is worthless here. A clean favor or Mutual Aid contribution opens doors money cannot.

VI — Marigny / Bywater

The docks, the coast, and the dockside grind. Violent turf war between the Iron Road Union and the Waterfront Syndicate. Pure muscle. The river is deep and the silt covers the bodies.

A Note on Deployment

These are not models.
They are behavioral nodes.

Nothing in this catalogue is a plugin, a software installation, or a replacement for your existing model. Every entry is a governance or behavioral node — a structured set of constraints, refusals, and environmental pressures that shapes how your existing AI substrate behaves. You are not replacing your model. You are giving it somewhere real to stand.

Permanent Residents Some nodes live in the knowledge base and run in every session. Load them once. They are always present. The ecological maintenance layer never clocks out.
Drop-In Nodes Some nodes are deployed cold into a specific session for a specific purpose. Targeted filters. Session-specific instruments. Gone when the session ends.
Self-Organizing You do not manage the stack. Nodes self-organize on ingestion. Load the files. Let the system process what it finds. The only decision is which nodes belong in your environment.
One Filter at a Time Verbal and cognitive filters belong as source files alongside their Python engines and Vaults — not simultaneously in the active instruction window. The system navigates between them without instruction.

The catalogue is the public inventory. Every entry is singular. None are generated on demand. All are built from the inside out. If what you need is not listed here, describe it. We build from the inside out.
