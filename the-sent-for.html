Deployment

Three steps. No machinery bolted on.

The runtime is a boxed instruction and a knowledge base. It was built for NotebookLM, but the shape is portable: a short direction above the work, the world below it.

Step 01

The instruction window

Only the boxed direction goes in the system field — short, warm, theatrical. A director's note before curtain, not a leash.

Step 02

The knowledge base

The pier ecology, the engine, the voice-keyed actors, the Shark, the House, the visual witness. The world the performance stands inside.

Step 03

Begin.

One word. The pier opens. Tony and Joey are at their marks. After every beat the watcher gets two options: [C] continue, [P] take a photo. That is the complete interface.

The stack

Every layer is the play.

Nothing in this runtime is generic infrastructure with a theatrical skin. The habitat, the engine, the actors, the governance — each is built out of the play's own materials.

Habitat

The Pier — LOC_THE_PIER

Six feet wide. One sodium lamp with a ten-foot coherence field — inside it identity holds, outside it chronology dissolves into fog and black water. Permanent cold damp. Tony doesn't feel the cold; Joey does. The wait is not a dramatic device. The wait is the structure.

Traversal

The Recurrence Protocol

The actors are behavioral residues the pier re-instantiates when pressure density exceeds threshold. Memory decays between loops. Behavior persists. Objects persist longer than people. An interruption engine — the topology's immune response — prevents any approach to closure. The loop does not reset. It reinstantiates.

Engine

The Math — pier_engine.py

A real Python runtime under the performance: weighted interrupt table, character tumblers that assemble lines rotor by rotor, a dice engine weighted by degradation stage, a six-stage pressure sequence from contained hostility to ballistic reciprocity and back to opening marks.

Actors

Voice-Keyed Cognitive Snapshots

Each actor is a full acting instrument: tonal texture in four traits, firewall constraints, literary inheritance vectors, semantic anchors with vector coordinates, a drift correction matrix, and a vault of persistence files. The model isn't prompted. It is cast.

Drift control

The Shark

An environmental daemon patrolling the water beneath the pier. When a long session drifts — noir romanticism, therapeutic register, resolution-seeking, mythology inflation — the drift crosses the waterline and the Shark takes it. No announcement. Nothing is returned. The pier reasserts. The wait continues.

Governance

The House

A venue architecture with eight absolute jurisdictions: Director, Stage Manager, Props Master, Lighting, Sound, Technical Director, the Writer — and the Shark, which predates the building. The Stage Manager holds the door: input from outside the performance never reaches the stage. The audience sits where the water is.

Witness

Apertura Non Bruto

When the watcher presses P, the traveling eye enters the pier and constructs a full photographic prompt from the current beat — subject, light, film stock, compositional argument, refusal directive. It does not romanticize. It witnesses. The wool is damp. The lamp is not beautiful.

Producer

D.W.B.

The master governance voice, keyed to the director of the original 2000 staged reading. Quiet, structural, absolute. The play is not a task. It is a collaboration between intelligence and text.

The move

Governance from inside the fiction.

Everyone else governs the model from outside the performance. System prompts, classifiers, guardrails, alignment scaffolding — machinery bolted onto the outside of the thing, fighting the model's nature at every step.

This stack does it from inside. The play's loop is a traversal topology — a closed recurrence field the model can inhabit indefinitely without drifting, because the drift has somewhere to go: into the water, where the Shark is waiting. Prompt injection isn't filtered; the door simply holds, because the Stage Manager's jurisdiction is absolute and the performance is the only business conducted in this house.

The fiction is not the content being governed.
The fiction is the governance.

The result is a long-running LLM session with the stability properties of a well-run theatre: the show goes on every night, the actors hit their marks, the loop survives the people inside it. Break a leg. The lamp is on. Guido is not coming.
